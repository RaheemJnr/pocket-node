v1.7.2:
Critical security fix. Wallets created on v1.7.0/v1.7.1 had reduced biometric protection on send signing, seed reveal, and sub-account derivation. v1.7.2 restores it.

Adding a sub-account now requires two unlock prompts. Both protect distinct operations.

If multiple wallets are being upgraded, cancelling one unlock prompt no longer blocks the others. Cancelled wallets are listed so you can retry them later.

Wallets created on v1.6.x and earlier are unaffected by this fix.

Source: github.com/RaheemJnr/pocket-node/releases
