v1.8.0:
Easier network switching, a fix for finding funds on protected wallets, and security improvements.

Switch networks in one tap. Tap the CKB Mainnet or Testnet chip on the home screen to switch, with a quick confirm. No more going into Settings.

Find funds on your other addresses, even on protected wallets. If your recovery phrase was used in another wallet, the scan that locates those funds, and the one-tap move to your main wallet, now work correctly when your wallet is protected by a fingerprint or device PIN.

Your PIN attempts reset after an app update. Updating no longer leaves you stuck at a low "attempts remaining" from before.

Stronger protection for your recovery phrase and private key. On a phone with no screen lock, viewing them now asks for your app PIN first. And if the phone has no fingerprint, PIN, or pattern at all, the app clearly warns you before storing keys in software-only mode.
