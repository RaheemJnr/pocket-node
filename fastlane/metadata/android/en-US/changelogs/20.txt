v1.7.9:
This release fixes wallets that hold more than 100 coins, so your full balance shows and every coin can be spent.

Wallets with many coins now show the correct balance. Underneath the app, the light client returned only the first 100 coins per request and stopped there, so a wallet with more than 100 small coins (mining payouts, frequent deposits) showed too low a balance and could only spend part of it. The app now reads every page of coins, so the full balance appears and a send can gather as many coins as it needs. A test send on a 183 coin wallet spent 151 of them in a single transaction.

Sends from busy wallets go through. A send that gathered many small coins paid slightly too little network fee and was rejected every time. Fees now cover the real transaction size.

The app can find funds from other wallets. If your seed phrase was used in Neuron or another wallet, funds can sit on addresses Pocket Node did not scan before. Importing now scans those addresses automatically, and Settings has a Scan Other Addresses action for existing wallets. Found funds are shown with their amounts, and a Move to main wallet button brings them to your main address in one transaction.

Activity shows your full history. The All tab previously stopped filling in on busy wallets, and an entry could show a wrong amount. The complete history is now cached with exact amounts.

Source: github.com/RaheemJnr/pocket-node/releases
