v1.7.8:
This release fixes sending from wallets with many small coins and finds funds on addresses other wallets used.

Sends from busy wallets go through. A send that gathered many small coins (mining payouts, frequent deposits) paid slightly too little network fee and was rejected every time. Fees now cover the real transaction size.

The app can find funds from other wallets. If your seed phrase was used in Neuron or another wallet, funds can sit on addresses Pocket Node did not scan before. Importing now scans those addresses automatically, and Settings has a Scan Other Addresses action for existing wallets. Found funds are shown with their amounts, and a Move to main wallet button brings them to your main address in one transaction.

Activity shows your full history. The All tab previously stopped filling in after roughly the newest fifty entries on busy wallets, and an entry could show a wrong amount. The complete history is now cached with exact amounts.
